TITLE: Water Dispenser 12-Step Chemical Sanitization Checklist
APPLICABILITY: Cairn BOTH | DEPT: Housekeeping & Hygiene | FREQ: weekly
TOTAL CHECKPOINTS: 13
  1. Step 1: Select the dispenser to be cleaned
  2. Step 2: Remove the empty jar
  3. Step 3: Remove the top & inner accessories
  4. Step 4: Drain the washes water from the hot and cold nozzel completely
  5. Step 5: Add sumatab solution of 150ppm & allow contact time of 15 minutes [Photo Required]
  6. Step 6: Manually clean the surface of inner with soft nylon brush
  7. Step 7: Use the fresh solution to clean the top & inner accessory
  8. Step 8: Rinse the top & inner accessory with potable water [Photo Required]
  9. Step 9: Pour a liter of potable water a long the wall side if inner surface
  10. Step 10: Again pour water along the wall side of inner surface
  11. Step 11: Drain the water from hot & cold nozzel completely
  12. Step 12: Replace the wash top and inner accessory
  13. Step 13: The jar is placed on the dispenser
